# CrossCheck Parts

## Boards
- Adafruit ESP32-S3 TFT Feather
- Adafruit KB2040

## Sensors
- LSM6DSOX + LIS3MDL 9-DoF IMU
- LSM6DSOX 6-DoF IMU
- TSSP77038 IR demodulator
- TSOP38238 IR receiver
- DRV5032 hall effect sensor

## Emitters and actuators
- 940 nm IR LED
- Rare earth magnet
- Vibrating mini motor disc

## Driver components
- PN2222 NPN transistor
- 1N4001 diode
- Resistors: 100 Ω, 220 Ω, 1 kΩ, 2.2 kΩ, 4.7 kΩ, 10 kΩ

## Power
- LiPo 3.7 V 500 mAh

## Assembly
- Half-size breadboard
- FeatherWing Proto
- STEMMA QT / Qwiic 4-pin cable
- STEMMA JST PH 3-pin cable
- Jumper wires M/M
- Jumper wires F/M
